MultiEcho workbench

Reports

Every sealed report in the workspace, from the workbench's runs or from your own scripts. All 22 conformance checks are re-run when a report is opened; a report whose seal does not match its content is marked.

Two experiments are comparable when every locked protocol field agrees (stimulus, interventions, contexts, observation time, keyframe policy, ...); subjects, maskers and methods may vary.

Keyframes

The frames a learned world received, kept by runs that declare kept frames. Under a keyframe policy, the keyframe of every branch is compared with its context's reference branch.
